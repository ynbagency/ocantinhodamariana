# Brand Direction

O Cantinho da Mariana

Internal territory: **Uma casa daqui. Com sabores que vieram de longe.**
Campaign direction: **Comida boa. Conversas melhores.**

Principles: warm, human, simple, recognisable, contemporary and culturally
distinctive without folkloric overload.

Staging palette:
- Burgundy #5B1715 / #7A1E1E
- Deep green #245B38
- Terracotta #C46A3A
- Cream #F7F0E4
- Beige #E8D6B8
- Brown #4B2E1F

AI raster concepts are not production logos. Final identity must be supplied as
approved vector masters.
